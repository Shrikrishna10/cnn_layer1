## **4. FEEDING 16-BIT VALUES TO 8-BIT MULTIPLIER**

### The Problem:
After accumulation, you have 16-bit (or 20-bit) values, but your multiplier expects 8-bit inputs.

### Solution: **Don't feed ACC back to multiplier!**

The accumulator output should NEVER go back to the multiplier input. Here's why:
```
CORRECT DATAFLOW:
═════════════════

Multiply Phase:
  8-bit pixel × 8-bit weight → 16-bit product
  ↓
  Product feeds adder (not multiplier)

Accumulate Phase:
  16-bit product + 20-bit ACC → 20-bit new ACC
  ↓
  Stored in ACC register

Reduction Phase:
  20-bit ACC0 + 20-bit ACC1 → 20-bit result
  (This uses a separate 20-bit adder, NOT the multiplier)

═══════════════════════════════════
  
  20-bit ACC → fed back to 8-bit multiplier input ✗

```

### Key Insight:
- **Multiplier inputs:** Always 8-bit × 8-bit (pixels × weights)
